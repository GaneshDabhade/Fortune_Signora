Help & support

Consumer help centres assume an anonymous visitor with a general question. Enterprise support is the opposite: the person has a contract with a response-time commitment, an environment the vendor already knows, and usually an error they hit ninety seconds ago. Nothing here asks someone to describe their own account back to us — every route into support arrives carrying the workspace, the plan, the screen, and the correlation ID.
