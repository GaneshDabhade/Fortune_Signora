Billing & subscription

Enterprise billing is not a checkout. It is an annual commitment with a true-up, a renewal date with a notice period, and a finance team that needs a PO number on an invoice. This module is designed so nobody is surprised: every figure that will appear on an invoice is shown before it does, and the two moments that actually damage trust — a failed payment and an unnoticed renewal — are treated as first-class states rather than emails.

08 · Permission, loading & error
Not a billing admin
You can see this, but not change it

Most people who open billing cannot act on it. Rather than an access-denied page, the whole module is readable with every control disabled and one route out: the two people who can.

Request a change
Usage and seat figures stay visible to every admin — a team that cannot see its own consumption cannot manage it.
Loading
Contract terms render immediately from the local record; only metered usage shimmers, because it is the only figure fetched live. A billing page that shows nothing for two seconds reads as an outage.
Errors
Usage figures are 6 hours stale

The metering service is behind. The figures shown are correct as of 02:14 and are marked with their timestamp rather than presented as current. Contract, seats and invoices are unaffected.

Card update failed

Your bank declined the verification charge of €0.00. Nothing was saved and your existing method is unchanged. The processor's reason is quoted verbatim — "issuer does not support zero-value authorisation" — because a friendlier rewrite sends people to the wrong place.
