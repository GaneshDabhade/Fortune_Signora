The copilot is everywhere, and it is always accountable

Ubiquity is the easy half. The hard half is that a model which appears on every screen must obey the same contract on every screen: it inherits the scope of what you are looking at, it cites what it read, it states how sure it is, it proposes rather than commits, and everything it does is attributable and reversible. This document specifies the seventeen surfaces and the one contract they share.

02

Inline AI & context AI

The copilot comes to the cursor, not the other way round

Inline AI operates on what is under the cursor — a cell, a field, a paragraph, a selection — and returns something you can accept, edit or reject in place. Context AI is the same engine offered from the object's own affordances: a row menu, a right-click, a hover on a metric. Neither one navigates you away, and neither one commits.

05

Agent tasks & multi-step actions

A multi-step action is the copilot doing several things in sequence on your behalf. The rule is that the plan is shown before it runs, each step declares what it may touch, anything that writes or sends stops for approval, and the run continues in the background so you are never watching a progress bar. Tasks are objects: they have an owner, a state, a history and an undo window.

Memory

Inspectable, editable, forgettable

A copilot that remembers is more useful and more dangerous. You can correct an entry, and correcting one is itself remembered so the same wrong inference is not re-derived next week.
