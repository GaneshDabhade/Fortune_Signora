First-time onboarding

Fifteen facts are needed to personalise the platform. Asking for fifteen would lose a third of the people who reached this point, so the design asks for four and infers the rest — from the email domain, the enrichment record behind it, the browser, and the answers already given. Every inferred value is shown, marked as inferred, and one click from being corrected.

The flow

Six steps, one required. The workspace exists after step 2 — everything after it refines a product the user already owns, which is why skipping is safe and why the completion screen can show real data rather than a congratulation.
