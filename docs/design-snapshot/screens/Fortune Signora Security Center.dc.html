Security Center

A security page has one job on the day it matters: let someone who thinks they have been compromised understand what happened and stop it, in under a minute, while frightened. Everything here is arranged for that reading — the unrecognised device is at the top, every destructive control states its blast radius before it fires, and no screen claims an outcome the system cannot guarantee.

04 · Account recovery

If you lose everything

Recovery is the weakest link in every account system: it is, by definition, the path that works without your factors. Signora treats it as an attack surface rather than a convenience, so each route states what it proves and how long it takes.
