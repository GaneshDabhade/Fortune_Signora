Team & invitation management

Access management fails in the same three places at every company: people are invited faster than they are removed, roles are granted permanently for a temporary reason, and nobody knows what a role actually permits until something leaks. This module answers each one directly — every permission shows where it came from, every removal names what the person owns, and every invitation carries an expiry it cannot outlive.
