Home

One route, four compositions, fourteen states. Home is the destination for every authenticated entry that is not a deep link, and its job is to answer one question in under five seconds: what should I do now. This document owns which cards appear, in what order, and what each renders when its data is absent, partial, forbidden or broken. It does not own routing, the shell, or any card's internal screens.

02 · Purpose, hierarchy & non-goals

Purpose

Home tells a person what to do next, using the workspace's own data, with a reason attached to every item. It is not a report, not a dashboard for its own sake, and not a menu. If a person would routinely click straight past Home to reach real work, Home has failed — and the correct response is to fix the composition, not to redirect them somewhere else.

The five-second test
Within five seconds of Home painting, a person must be able to name the one thing they should do first. Every card either contributes to that answer or does not belong in the composition.

03 · The prohibition — Home never becomes another surface

Home must never fall back to a different product surface because it has no data

An empty Home that redirects to the Attention list, the account table, or any other surface produces an alternate Home that no document describes, no test covers, and no person can predict. A designed empty state is strictly preferable. The rule is absolute and applies to every composition and every state in this document: Home always renders Home.
