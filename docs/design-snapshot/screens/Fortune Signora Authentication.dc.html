Authentication

The first screen an enterprise buyer sees, and the one their security team reads most carefully. Every state here is designed to be legible under stress: what happened, whether it was you, and exactly what to do next. No screen blames the user, none leaks whether an account exists, and none is a dead end.

Re-authentication happens over the page, not instead of it. The route, scroll position, filters and draft state survive; the underlying content is dimmed and inert but still visible, so people can see what they were doing. A full redirect is used only when the session is revoked rather than expired.

Responsive behaviour

The card is the layout, so there is almost nothing to reflow. Below 480px it stops being a card and becomes the page — the border, radius and shadow are dropped rather than shrunk, which is what keeps it from looking like a website on a phone.
